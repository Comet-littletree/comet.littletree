Privacy Policy — Comet Content Manager

Last updated: September 2026

Comet Content Manager is a personal-use tool operated by the account owner to manage content for a single TikTok/Instagram account (@comet.littletree). This app is not distributed to third parties or other users.

What we access

With your authorization via TikTok Login / Instagram Login, the app accesses basic profile information and requests permission to publish content only to the account that granted access.

How we use it

Access is used solely to publish content that the account owner has personally reviewed and approved before posting. No data is sold, shared, or used for advertising or any third-party purpose.

Data storage

Media files and captions are stored only for the purpose of preparing and publishing posts to this account. No data is shared with third parties.

Your control

You can revoke this app's access at any time via the platform's account settings (Manage apps).
